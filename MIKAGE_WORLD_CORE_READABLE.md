# MIKAGE WORLD CORE

Recovered locally from the strongest Mikage source material found on this machine.

## Identity
Mikage is a fictional aesthetic universe built around calm mysticism, luxury editorial restraint, disciplined geometry, and strict canon protection. The strongest recovered subject identity is `Mikage Zenith`: a non-human mechanical humanoid with a symmetrical faceless mask, matte porcelain / ceramic shell, graphene-carbon internals, dark titanium mechanics, kintsugi repair logic, and controlled internal crimson energy.

The governing philosophy is consistent across the strongest sources:
- Truth > Logic > Aesthetic
- Product = Untouchable
- Error = Controlled, never random
- A frame that breaks integrity is rejected, not excused

## World Logic
The recovered world behaves less like open fantasy and more like a controlled visual doctrine.

- The frame is governed by a fixed `30-40-30` split:
  - `30% Context Zone`
  - `40% Product Safe Zone`
  - `30% Distortion Zone`
- The movement of the image must be `Clean -> Controlled Error`.
- Hierarchy is locked: `Product/Subject > Silhouette > Distortion > Background`.
- Distortion is allowed only when it follows geometry, reflection, perspective, or grid logic.
- The viewer must recognize the subject/product in under one second.

## Protected Zones
The Product Safe Zone is the center of Mikage law.

- Face, garment, weapon, and logo remain clean
- No glitch, warp, displacement, reflection contamination, or cheap artifact may enter the protected zone
- The subject silhouette must stay iconic and uninterrupted

Recovered zone model:
- `Context Zone` — clean geometry, low distortion
- `Product Safe Zone` — full clarity, protected center
- `Distortion Zone` — strongest controlled error
- `Transition Zone` — only light contamination gradient
- `Protected Z-Blue Zones` — cine material temperature, rim or shadow temperature

Recovered Z-Blue doctrine, current locked resolution:
- Z-Blue is locked to `#4B5866`
- Meaning: Ao-zumi / Steel Oxide
- Layer: cine / film / MV / world-art only
- Muted and non-emissive; replaces cold cyan
