## Material Review

PASS for formal visual-material review.

- Porcelain helmet quality: V0.2 preserves the faceted white helmet identity and improves the V0.1 surface read with subtler premium facet planes, cleaner micro-beveled edge behavior, and less placeholder-like paneling. The close-up view reads as matte porcelain/gofun rather than toy plastic or glossy sci-fi armor.
- Void-black body quality: the body remains a dominant vertical black mass with controlled grey value separation. V0.2 ribs and inset seams reduce the flat blockout read while keeping the surface quiet and non-organic.
- Restrained violet slit signal: violet remains concentrated in the two horizontal sensor slits. The signal reads as the sanctioned slit halo/focal system rather than decorative fill.
- Z-Blue compliance: no spectral or neon Z-Blue drift is visible in the proof. Any cool support remains muted and non-emissive in the `#4B5866` direction described by the cine color contract.
- V0.1 to V0.2 improvement: V0.2 improves the three V0.1 weaknesses called out in review: arms/hands integration, torso flatness, and helmet surface polish.

## Silhouette Review

PASS for formal silhouette review.

- Full-body read: the character reads quickly as a faceless white porcelain helmet over a black vertical body mass with a separate heavy blade slab.
- Black vertical body mass: preserved. The added ribs and inset seams do not interrupt the monolithic body read.
- Helmet/body relationship: preserved and clearer than V0.1. The faceted white helmet remains the primary upper focal form without becoming a human face.
- Arm/hand readability: improved vs V0.1. The visible-presentation mitten-hand forms, wrist shadows, and shoulder/arm bridge pieces reduce chunky placeholder read while keeping the forms simple.
- Zenith Blade scale/readability: preserved. The blade remains a tall vertical slab beside the body with strong scale and clear separation from the torso.
