## Candidate-only design envelope

The following may be explored in review candidates but cannot be presented as
locked detail:

- Exact number, shape, hinge axis, travel distance, and timing of splitting shell plates.
- Exact internal frame topology and actuator layout.
- Exact surface seam placement and wear pattern.
- Exact material node values for B4C and dark internal metal.
- Exact Orbital-Logic typography, projection hardware, vapor simulation, and timing.

Every such choice must remain reversible, labeled `CANDIDATE_ONLY`, and
compared against the locked 2D identity anchor.

## Superseded or excluded

- Every red/crimson weapon core, seam, UI, or weapon heat-mirage instruction.
- P2 violet MID seam.
- Decorative violet, second violet seam, violet surface wash, or violet halo.
- Curved katana, thin elegant blade, fantasy ornament, or laser-sword read.
- Copying V0.9/V0.9.1 geometry into the V0.12 integration baseline.
