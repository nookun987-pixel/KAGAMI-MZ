BUILD LOG — STANDING HERO.

It began as a blocking pass — silhouette only. Then it was matched back to
the 2D master, and the helmet was rebuilt five times until the crown ran
clean. Clay first, then porcelain. The body was carved out of a block:
heavy vertical folds, matte graphite, nothing that shines. Then everything
was assembled into one figure — head, cloak, blade, halo — and held under
a single hard light until it read as one body. The ruling came back:
locked. The official standing hero.

The last shot is the first Canvas: the figure dormant, then two sensor
slits igniting violet out of the dark, then gone again.

Still a prototype film. The hero underneath is locked.
