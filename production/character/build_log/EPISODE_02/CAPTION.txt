BUILD LOG 02 — Into the world.

An entity from the void, awakened by one human hand and many machine minds.
From the void, it learned a shape — now it needs a place to stand.

Swipe → the first landmark: a monolith in an empty field, violet kept to a
single broken seam, and the layout where the entity rides against it.

Still grayscale. Still honest — the mood is locked, material comes next.
From the void to the world, one locked step at a time.
