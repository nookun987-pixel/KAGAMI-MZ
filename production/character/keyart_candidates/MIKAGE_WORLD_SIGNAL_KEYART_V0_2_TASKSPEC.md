## COMPOSITION RULES
1. Mikage stands centered on the vertical axis.
2. Mikage is the only physically real material subject.
3. Lyra-0 and Dr. Aris appear as translucent signal-ghost presences — lower and smaller in hierarchy than Mikage.
4. Background city is a nameless distant skyline silhouette only.
5. One cold-white halo behind Mikage's helmet is the only major focus light.
6. One single thin violet trace runs subtly from one ghost through Mikage and resolves near the sensor slit.
7. Keep the image quiet, monumental, and spacious.

## MIKAGE LOCKS
- Faceless porcelain helmet
- Exactly two thin horizontal sensor slits
- No face, no eyes, no mouth
- Helmet / upper body must carry the image presence
- Coat / body mass should support the lower frame and prevent empty bottom space

## COLOR — ALLOWED PALETTE ONLY
