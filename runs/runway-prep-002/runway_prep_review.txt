verdict: BORDERLINE

strengths
- centered hero framing is immediate and readable in under one second
- silhouette is clean and commercially legible for a premium opening frame
- no obvious neon RGB clutter, anime saturation, or magenta ambient contamination
- frontal symmetry is stronger than prior rejects and the frame feels controlled

fail points
- mask integrity is still off: the face reads as a rounded visor shell, not a sharp sacred kitsune mask with planar forehead and cheek planes
- material fidelity is still too smooth and fashion-fabric-like; the white shell does not read strongly enough as matte B4C ceramic with eggshell microtexture and subtle fracture language
- blade requirement is not satisfied in the image; the massive straight engineered blade is effectively absent from the keyframe
- brutalist environment is too neutral and minimal; it does not yet establish a strong sacred-tech monolith context
- validator hard fails remain live: T5, T6, T11, T12
- semantic canon layer is not fully confirmable because blocking unknown rules remain: I1, I2, I3, I4, I5, I6, C2

best use in ad
- usable as an internal moodboard or previsual direction frame for Runway camera/motion testing
- not usable yet as final canon-approved source image for production Runway prep
