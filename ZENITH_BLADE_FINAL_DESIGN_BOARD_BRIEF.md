## Summary of what blocks proceeding

Before any of the "YES — new render needed" items above can be produced meaningfully, five things from the Draft Bible's §16 should be resolved, in this order of leverage:

1. **Operator ruling on the ce15 silhouette/cohesion candidate** — everything downstream (hero shots, transformation strip, closeups) depends on knowing which candidate is final.
2. **Chassis structural-description conflict** (production "spine+rails+hub+base+collars" vs. this-session "two-lobe chassis, central slot") — blocks the exploded load-path diagram (#7) and the core/spine section (#8) from being drawn correctly.
3. **Porcelain hex-value conflict** (`#FAFAFA` vs `#F2EEEA`) — blocks material chips (#11) and the color-value card (#12).
4. **Dimension cross-check** (canon inch-range vs. built 1.2m) — blocks the dimensioned drawing (#14).
5. **Lineage-merge decision** (production pipeline vs. this-session ce01–ce15) — affects which source images are treated as authoritative for panels #1–#3, #6, #9–#10.

Items #4 (rear 3/4), #7 (exploded diagram), #8 (section cutaway), #13 (scale comparison), #15 (motion arrows), and #16 (callouts) have **no usable existing source in either lineage** and will require new work regardless of how the conflicts above resolve — they are the genuine gaps, not merely blocked-pending-ruling items.
