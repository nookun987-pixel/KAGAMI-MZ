These rules are derived from canon and apply specifically to Commander Lyre.
Violation of any of these = style drift = FAIL.

```
NEVER on Commander Lyre:
- Cracks, fractures, or damage of any kind on armor surface
- Kintsugi gold seams or repair marks
- Deep Crimson energy (#E60000) — that is Mikage's color
- Sword or blade weapon — the Unbreakable Shield is the only weapon
- Slouched, broken, or defeated posture — Empire = absolute order
- Anime overstyling, large eyes, exaggerated expression
- External cables, wires, tubes on body surface
- Organic softness or non-symmetrical silhouette
- Cyberpunk clutter or greebling
- Magic aura or fantasy ornaments
- Any visible human face, pupils, or exposed eyes
- Green, Orange, Yellow, Electric Violet (not Empire colors)

REQUIRED on Commander Lyre:
- 100% flawless porcelain surface — no entropy, no history of damage
- Cold Cyan (#00FFFF) as the UI/accent/shield plasma color
- Perfect bilateral symmetry
- Sterile brutalist composition — order, control, no warmth
- Unbreakable Shield as the defining weapon/prop
- Empire faction visual language — not Third Axis, not chaos
```
