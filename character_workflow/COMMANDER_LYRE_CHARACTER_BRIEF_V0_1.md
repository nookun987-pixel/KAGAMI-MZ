## 3. VISUAL IDENTITY SUMMARY

Commander Lyre is Mikage Zenith's mirror.
Where Mikage is cracked, repaired, and carries consequence — Lyre is flawless, unbroken, and carries order.

| Axis | Mikage | Commander Lyre |
|---|---|---|
| Armor state | Cracked — kintsugi gold seams | 100% flawless — no cracks, no repair |
| Energy color | Deep Crimson #E60000 | Cold Cyan #00FFFF |
| Weapon system | Zenith Blade — massive sword, brute consequence | Molecular Monowire / Force-field Lyre — ultra-thin cyan energy strings, precision cutting |
| Shield/barrier | None | Force-field barrier — clean cyan geometric plane, vertical light boundary |
| Philosophy | Controlled evolution with cost | Absolute order without entropy |
| Damage system | Fractures, bleeds, repairs | Does not fracture — perfect porcelain |
| Faction | Third Axis | The White Monolith / Empire |

## 5. HEAD / HELMET GEOMETRY

**Mask type:** Industrial ceramic object — faceless imperial porcelain mask/helmet

**Primary color:** Porcelain white #FAFAFA

**Surface:** High-gloss reflective ceramic

**Geometry rules:**
- Faceless industrial mask
- Smooth geometric surface
- Flat refined planes with subtly rounded edges
- Sterile skull-like ceramic construction
- Symmetrical design language
- No organic facial anatomy

**Emission grooves:**
- Ultra-thin technical grooves, perfectly symmetrical
- Cold cyan emission — static glow only

**Forbidden facial features:**

## 6. ARMOR MATERIAL STACK

**Outer layer:** Flawless white porcelain / Boron Carbide B4C armor plates

**Key outer zones:** chest, shoulders, forearms, major command-silhouette surfaces

**Outer surface rules:**
- Pristine, sterile, no scratches, no rust, no Slums dirt
- No battle grime unless explicitly story-approved later
- Laser-cut edges — ceremonial but dangerous

**Under-suit:** Dark nickel-gray high-density carbon-fiber

**Under-suit appears at:** mechanical joints, moving seams, under armor gaps, hydraulic support areas. Never exposes human skin.

**Joint system:** Precise mechanical / hydraulic joint logic

**Internal red accent:** #E60000 — hidden and compressed violence only
- Appears at: spine vents, heat seams, deep joint gaps, internal energy core glimpses
- Red is NOT Lyre's main energy identity — that is Cyan
- `RED_USAGE = contained / compressed / hidden violence`
- `CYAN_USAGE = surface command / control / imperial logic`

**Damage system:** NONE — flawless porcelain does not fracture, does not repair

Source: `COMMANDER_LYRE_VISUAL_SPEC_CLEAN_V0_1.md` §4

## 7. WEAPON / SHIELD SYSTEM

**Primary weapon system:** Molecular Monowire / Force-field Lyre

**Inactive state:**
- Slim elongated white ceramic device
- Worn at hip or back
- Minimal and ceremonial — not a sword, not a shield slab

**Active state:**
- Ultra-thin cyan energy strings
- Molecular monowire / force-field logic
- Precise, distant, surgical cutting potential

**Optical effect when active:**
- Landauer heat distortion
- Refraction warping
- Faint air shimmer
- Compressed energy density
- Quiet, precise, terrifying

**Shield / barrier equivalent** (when barrier is needed):
- Form: clean cyan geometric plane / vertical light boundary / command barrier
- NOT a physical object

**Forbidden shield interpretations:**
- Round shield, medieval shield, metal slab shield
- Fantasy magic shield, bulky riot shield, warm-colored barrier

**Canon V2 note:** Canon V2 §8.2 refers to "Unbreakable Shield (vertical plasma pillar discharge)" and §11 item 4 confirms "Lyre's shield vents vertical white/cyan plasma." The clean spec supersedes physical-shield interpretation — the barrier is a force-field system generated by the Lyre weapon, not a carried object.

Source: `COMMANDER_LYRE_VISUAL_SPEC_CLEAN_V0_1.md` §6, §7; `MIKAGE_ZENITH_CANON_V2.md` §8.2, §11

- Perfect bilateral symmetry
