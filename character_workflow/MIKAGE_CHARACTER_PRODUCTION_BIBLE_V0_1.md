## 1. CHARACTER IDENTITY RULE

Mikage Zenith is a porcelain executor / ceremonial synthetic warrior.

Required identity traits:
- faceless porcelain helmet
- exactly two ultra-narrow horizontal sensor slits
- no visible human face
- no visible human eyes
- no visible lips
- no visible skin

Meaning:
- the helmet is not a visor with many lines
- the mask is not expressive like a human face
- the character must feel cold, sacred, severe, and premium

---

## 2. SILHOUETTE RULE

Primary silhouette traits:
- long heavy black hair
- broad shoulders
- narrow waist, but not sexualized
- ceremonial, monolithic, calm stance
- female-coded silhouette without glamor/fanservice emphasis

Keep:
- clear top-heavy sacred silhouette
- strong shoulder / torso block
- long vertical line flow through hair / armor / skirt panels

Do not drift into:
- anime idol silhouette
- pin-up pose
- exaggerated chest/hip emphasis
- light playful action silhouette

---

## 3. MATERIAL / COLOR RULE

Allowed material system:
- main armor: porcelain white
- underlayer: black graphene / black technical under-suit
- accents: electric violet only

Allowed palette logic:
- white + black + violet
- cold neutral rendering
- no warm colors

Do not add:
- red, orange, gold-dominant, pink-dominant palettes
- rainbow accent systems
- muddy grey-brown armor drift

---

## 4. HELMET RULE

Helmet must remain:
- faceless
- smooth porcelain surface
- exactly two horizontal sensor slits
- clean and minimal

Forbidden helmet drift:
- more than two slits
- vertical eye shapes
- visible pupils / eyes
- mouth opening
- human nose / lips
- busy sci-fi visor clutter

Priority drift check order:
1. slit count
2. slit shape
3. face visibility
4. surface cleanliness

Armor language:
- porcelain white outer plates
- black segmented underlayer visible at joints/gaps
- precise violet accents only
- ceremonial tech-knight logic

Armor mood:
- premium
- austere
- engineered
- sacred / ritualistic

Do not drift into:
- over-busy mecha clutter
- medieval fantasy ornament overload
- exposed body/fanservice armor
- casual clothing / streetwear

## 6. PROP RULE — ZENITH BLADE

Zenith Blade must remain:
- a massive rectangular slab sword
- monolithic and heavy
- not a katana
- not a thin longsword
- not a decorative fantasy scimitar

Keep:
- rectangular silhouette
- heavy block mass
- dark blade body
- minimal violet detail accents if needed

Forbidden drift:
- curved blade
- elegant samurai katana shape
- tiny/light sword proportions
- over-ornamented fantasy guard
