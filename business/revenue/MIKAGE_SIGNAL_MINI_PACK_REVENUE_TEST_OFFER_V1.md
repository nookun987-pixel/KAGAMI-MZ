MIKAGE SIGNAL MINI PACK

## 4. Target buyer

Primary target:
- small YouTubers
- TikTok / Reels creators
- AI content pages
- indie game developers
- dark / cyber / anime visual pages
- creators who need intro / outro / sound identity direction

Do not target large brands first.
Do not pitch as a full agency service yet.

## 5. Core promise

Turn a project into a short signal identity:
- sonic direction
- short-form hook direction
- caption angle
- visual direction
- basic usage note

## 6. Pricing ladder

### 5 USD — Signal Line

Deliverables:
- 1 short tagline or caption direction
- 1 sonic mood direction
- 1 visual direction

No final audio delivery.
No video delivery.

Use when:
- buyer has low trust
- buyer only wants a quick direction
- first-contact test

### 9 USD — Signal Tag

Deliverables:
- 1 short intro / sound tag concept, 3-5 seconds
- 1 caption
- 1 visual prompt or card direction
- 1 simple usage note

Use when:
- buyer wants a small identity idea
- no heavy production required

### 19 USD — Short Hook Starter

Deliverables:
- 1 hook direction, 10-15 seconds
- 1 music generation / production prompt
- 3 caption options
- 1 vertical short visual direction
- 1 basic rights / usage note

Use when:
- buyer wants a short-form post concept
- buyer needs enough material to test content

### 29 USD — Creator Signal Mini Pack

Deliverables:
- 1 hook direction, 15-20 seconds
- 1 vertical short script or timed text outline
- 3 captions
- 1 cover / card direction
- 1 basic proof / usage note

Use when:
- buyer has an active page or project
- buyer wants a compact package, not only idea text

## 7. What this offer is NOT

This is not:
- full music production
- full music video production
- guaranteed viral content
- guaranteed copyright clearance across all platforms
- a replacement for legal advice
- a finished commercial campaign

Do not overpromise.

## 12. Pass / fail condition

PASS if within 30 days:
- at least 1 paid order, OR
- at least 3 serious buyer conversations

FAIL if within 30 days:
- no serious reply
- no one understands the offer
- no one wants even the 5 USD tier

## 13. If fail

Do not raise price.
Do not create a bigger package.
Do not blame the market immediately.

Check:
1. Is the offer understandable in 5 seconds?
2. Is the sample/demo concrete enough?
3. Is the target buyer correct?
4. Is the output too vague?
5. Is the pitch too much lore and not enough result?

## 14. Upgrade rule

Only consider higher pricing after:
- 5 paid test buyers, OR
- 3 public case studies, OR
- 1 repeat buyer

Until then, do not set minimum price to 99 USD.
