## 3. THE DISPOSITION (in force — ruled 2026-08-06, signed 2026-08-07)

> ### V0.1 = **CINE ICON REFERENCE**
>
> 1. **Role granted.** The `BLADE_V0.1` set is the **official iconography / teaser layer language**
>    for the Zenith Blade: posters, glyphs, reveal teasers, key-art marks, merch marks, and any
>    stylised or symbolic depiction where the Blade functions as an *emblem* rather than as the
>    rendered object.
> 2. **Role NOT granted.** V0.1 is **not production canon**, **not a canon authority**, **not a
>    geometry source**, and **not a comparison target** for any build, proof, gate or ruling.
>    Nothing may cite it as ground truth.
> 3. **Self-declared locks VOID.** The "LOCKED" labels on the filename, the poster and the viewer
>    header are **void and of no effect**. No lock document for V0.1 ever existed. The labels are
>    left in place on the files as historical record — this document is what contradicts them.
> 4. **CE15 remains the sole production canon.** Weapon form, silhouette, seams, materials and core
>    behaviour are governed by [ZENITH_BLADE_CANON_LOCK_V1.md](ZENITH_BLADE_CANON_LOCK_V1.md)
>    (+ [ERRATA 01](ZENITH_BLADE_CANON_LOCK_V1_ERRATA_01.md)) and
>    [ZENITH_BLADE_MATERIAL_CANON_V1.md](ZENITH_BLADE_MATERIAL_CANON_V1.md). On any conflict between
>    V0.1 and CE15, **CE15 wins, without exception.**
> 5. **Divergence is expected, not an error.** V0.1's slim spike, mid-shaft grip ring, kintsugi-gold
>    cracks and crimson seams are **icon-layer language**. They are not defects to be reconciled and
>    they must not be back-ported into any build.
> 6. **Files unchanged.** No file in `assets/keyart/blade/` or in the external `BLADE_V0.1\` set is
>    edited, renamed, moved or deleted by this disposition.
> 7. **Publication status of the 17-file external set remains UNCONFIRMED.** This disposition assigns
>    a role; it does not authorise, retract, or confirm any past or future publication. See §6.
