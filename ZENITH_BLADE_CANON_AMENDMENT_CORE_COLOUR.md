## 1. Authoritative rule

**`#8F00FF` electric violet is the authoritative Zenith Blade core / seam signal colour.**

- Visible in **P3 only**. Exactly **one** core.
- **No** wash, halo, ambient fill, bloom, glare, secondary core, or decorative violet lighting.
- **Red / crimson is banned on the weapon at every phase (P1, P2, P3).**

Primary source:
> "the Zenith Blade core/seam signal is **electric violet, `#8F00FF` family** (rendered core-body median gate: hue 268–280°, R/B 0.45–0.65). Red/crimson is **BANNED** on this weapon at every phase."
> — `docs/handoff/MIKAGE_ZENITH_BLADE_SPEC_V1.md`, header banner, operator ruling 2026-07-06/07

Enforcement in current evidence (CE15 pass_03): P1 violet = 0 · P2 violet = 0 · P3 violet ROI = 2879 (gate ≥ 2500) · global violet 0.139 % (gate ≤ 5 %).
