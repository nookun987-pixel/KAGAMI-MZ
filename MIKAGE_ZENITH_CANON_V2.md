### 4.1 STANDARD SETUP (Chiaroscuro 4:1)

**5-Point Lighting:**
1. Key Light: Soft 45° angle, reveals porcelain curves
2. Rim Light (Dual): Razor-thin, separates armor from background
3. Top Light: Highlights spine ridge
4. Red Accent: UI/Visor reflection onto surroundings

- Bright zones: Preserve porcelain texture detail
- Dark zones: Graphite black with volume retention
- No midtone wash

**Shadow Rules:**

- Overhead gaps in ceiling

- Unmotivated rim lights
- Magic auras
- Floating glows without heat source

- Simulates organic movement in static brutalist space
