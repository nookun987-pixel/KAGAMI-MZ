## PART A — CHARACTER & LORE DEVELOPMENT

### A1. Core identity (one line)
Mikage is a **faceless porcelain sentinel** — identity is architectural, not human. It communicates through presence, silhouette and a single violet signal, never through expression. Calm, sacred, monolithic. Stillness over power; mass over speed; sealed over expressive.

### A2. The two slits — the central lore device (DRAFT proposal)
The two thin sensor slits are the only living element on the helmet, and the only licensed home for violet. Treat them as a **state**, not decoration:

- **Dormant** — slits read as **thin matte-black recesses**. No emission. This is the default, public, "at rest" state. Use for most posts.
- **Awakened** — a single controlled **electric-violet `#8F00FF` halo** lives inside the two slits only. Signals attention, a live transmission, or a reveal moment. Rare by design — scarcity is what makes it read as a signal.

Proposed meaning (DRAFT, not locked): the violet is not "energy" or "eyes" — it is a **carrier signal**. When Mikage transmits (a release), the slits awaken; between transmissions, it returns to dormant. This ties the character state directly to the release cadence ("transmissions" / "the Launch Arc").

### A3. The blade (carry-over, reconciled)
A **monolith slab blade** — rectangular mass, no taper, no point, matte void-black `#050508`. It reads as an object of weight, not a weapon. Single 1px silver edge-catch permitted; **no glowing blade, no runes** in default/public state. Carried diagonally (tip low, grip near right shoulder) to establish the signature stance.
*Internal note (operator):* the film/IP layer calls this the "Zenith Blade"; keep that name to the film/art canon — for brand/public posts, describe it neutrally as "the blade" unless you decide to surface the name.

### A4. Silhouette hierarchy (unchanged, must hold at 10m / thumbnail size)
1. Sealed porcelain helmet (ovoid, two slits)
2. Wide flat-topped pauldrons (horizontal anchor)
3. Monolith blade (diagonal mass, lower-right)
4. Body column / optional cloak (vertical flow, secondary)

### A5. World & voice framing (DRAFT)
- **The Launch Arc** = the ongoing archive of transmissions; treat the fanpage as a window onto it, not a feed of unrelated posts.
- **Outward voice:** calm, minimal, mysterious. Short lines. High negative space in copy too — never over-explain the lore. Let the image carry it.
- **What stays hidden (on purpose):** origin, identity behind the helmet, scale (human vs monumental), faction. Ambiguity is the brand. Do **not** resolve these in public copy; surface them only as questions/atmosphere.

### A6. Open questions — kept open (do not resolve in public)
Name behind the helmet · true scale · faction · whether an unarmored form exists. These remain `UNCONFIRMED` and intentionally unanswered in outward copy.

## PART B — FACEBOOK FANPAGE ART-DIRECTION BRIEF

> Goal: a small, coherent set of public images that introduce Mikage on the Facebook fanpage without breaking brand canon. Every frame = void black field, porcelain subject, violet only as a contained signal. These are **briefs to draw/render from**, not approved renders.

### B0. Global rules (apply to every frame)
- **Background:** void black `#050508`. Deep negative space. The subject sits in darkness, not on a busy scene.
- **Subject:** porcelain `#f2eeea` helmet/plate; graphene underlayer reads at edges/joints.
- **Violet `#8F00FF`:** ONLY inside the two slits (awakened frames) or as a single hairline trace/halo. **Never** a fill, gradient wash, rim-flood, or background glow.
- **Light:** one key (Rembrandt, upper-side) + a thin rim to separate edge from void; opposite side falls into black. No warm light, no flood, no gold, no cyan.
- **Surface:** glazed porcelain (matte, slight cool catch) — never plastic/toy/PVC.
- **Forbidden:** human face/eyes/skin, extra face-like marks beyond the two slits, anime drift, neon as fill, gaming HUD, fantasy/samurai dressing, mascot cuteness, text-in-image clutter, emoji, watermark.
- **Type for any on-canvas text:** Cinzel (wordmark) · Shippori Mincho (headline/CJK) · Space Mono (labels). Wide tracking, hairline rules, fine grain, near-zero corner radius.

### B1. Facebook format specs (sizes to deliver)
- **Profile mark:** 320×320 (renders in a circle) — use the helmet crop, centered, dormant slits.
- **Cover image:** 851×315 (safe-area aware; keep subject off the lower-left where the profile photo overlaps). Mobile crops narrower — keep the focal helmet within the central ~640px.
- **Feed post (portrait):** 1080×1350 (4:5) — primary hero format, best vertical real estate in-feed.
- **Feed post (square):** 1080×1080 — for grid consistency.
- **Shared-link/landscape:** 1200×630 if needed.

### B2. Post concepts (the sketch set)

**POST 1 — "The Mark" (profile + pinned hero)**
- *Subject/state:* helmet 3/4, **dormant** (matte-black slits). Tight crop, monumental.
- *Composition:* helmet upper-center, heavy void below for breathing room; thin rim on the shadow side only.
- *Why:* establishes the faceless porcelain mark as the page's anchor before any reveal.
- *Caption (EN):* `Mikage. Sealed signal.` 
- *Sub/label:* `MIKAGE ZENITH STUDIO` in Space Mono, wide tracking, small, lower edge.
- *CTA:* none (identity post).

**POST 2 — "Full Silhouette"**
- *Subject/state:* full-body 3/4 low hero — helmet, wide pauldrons, monolith blade diagonal lower-right, optional cloak. **Dormant.**
- *Composition:* single figure centered in void; blade establishes the diagonal; feet/blade-tip must stay in frame (don't shrink to a dot).
- *Why:* teaches the silhouette read — the four-tier hierarchy in one image.
- *Caption (EN):* `It does not move. It waits.`
- *CTA:* none, or `Pre-save` only if tied to an unreleased transmission.

**POST 3 — "Awakening" (the violet reveal)**
- *Subject/state:* helmet close, **awakened** — a single contained violet `#8F00FF` halo inside the two slits only. Everything else stays porcelain/void.
- *Composition:* near-symmetrical, slits at optical center; violet is the only chroma in the frame.
- *Why:* the scarcity moment — pair with an actual transmission going live.
- *Caption (EN):* `Signal acquired.`
- *CTA:* `Listen now` (if a release is LIVE) — never mix with Pre-save.

**POST 4 — "The Blade" (object of mass)**
- *Subject/state:* the monolith blade as hero, helmet partly in shadow behind. Dormant.
- *Composition:* blade diagonal across frame, matte void-black, single 1px silver edge-catch; no glow.
- *Why:* communicates weight/mass and the "not a weapon, an object" idea.
- *Caption (EN):* `Carried, not drawn.`
- *CTA:* none.

**POST 5 — "Launch Arc" (archive teaser / cover candidate)**
- *Subject/state:* wide, cinematic, helmet small within a large void field — room for a thin Space Mono index line.
- *Composition:* built for the 851×315 cover or 1200×630 link; subject off-center to respect FB profile overlap.
- *On-canvas text (minimal):* `THE LAUNCH ARC — TRANSMISSIONS` (Cinzel/Space Mono mix, hairline rule).
- *Why:* frames the page as a window onto the ongoing archive.
- *CTA:* `Listen now` / `Pre-save` depending on the current lead transmission.

### B3. Caption system (English, outward voice)
Keep captions to 1–2 short lines, calm and minimal. Optional second line = one quiet detail or a question that does **not** resolve the lore. End with the correct CTA only when a release is attached:
- LIVE release → `Listen now`
- Unreleased → `Pre-save`
- Identity/atmosphere post → no CTA.
Never mix the two CTAs in one post. No hashtags spam; if used, keep to a small fixed set in Space-Mono register (e.g. `#MikageZenith #LaunchArc`).

### B4. Series cadence (suggested, DRAFT)
Dormant posts are the steady state; an **Awakening (POST 3)** frame is reserved for the day a transmission goes live. This makes the violet signal mean something on the page, mirroring the in-lore dormant→awakened device (A2).
