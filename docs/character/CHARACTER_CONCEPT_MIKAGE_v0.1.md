## 0. Overview

Mikage is the central character identity of Mikage Zenith Studio.
The character communicates through **presence, silhouette, and sealed form** — not expression.
Identity is architectural, not humanized.

## 2. Silhouette Rules

The character must be readable as a distinct silhouette at 10m distance.

**Required reads:**
- Smooth sealed helmet — no facial protrusion, no horn, no crest break
- Wide armor shoulder plates (pauldrons) — significantly wider than head
- Massive diagonal rectangular slab sword anchoring lower-right quadrant
- Vertical body column: narrow torso, long drape, grounded stance

**Silhouette hierarchy:**
1. Helmet (sealed ovoid)
2. Pauldrons (horizontal width anchor)
3. Monolith sword (diagonal mass, lower right)
4. Body/cloak (vertical flow, secondary read)

**Hair / cloak** may extend the silhouette edge but must NOT become the primary shape.
Cloak is optional geometry — a shadow extension of armor, not a costume element.

---

## 3. Helmet Rules

> The helmet is the face. It is permanently sealed. It has no expression.

**Required:**
- Fully enclosed smooth ovoid form
- Material: porcelain white — matte, cool, slight surface catch of silver/light
- NO eye opening, NO visor, NO slit, NO mouth line, NO facial feature of any kind
- NO transparency, NO glowing eyes behind surface
- Subtle structural seams are permitted (vertical center seam, horizontal armor band) — these are panel construction lines, not facial cues
- A single seal glyph (circle motif, violet) may appear on lower face area — this is an emblem, not an eye

**Forbidden on helmet:**
- Eye slit or visor of any color (including cold blue, red, white)
- Mouth, chin strap, lower jaw opening
- Cracks that reveal an interior
- Organic skin texture beneath
- Expression of any kind

**Proportion:**
- Helmet should read as slightly oversized relative to body — monumental, not proportionally naturalistic
- Top ridge curve visible — smooth, not horned

---

## 4. Armor Rules

**Body armor:**
- White or near-white primary plate — same porcelain register as helmet
- Structural seams and panel lines permitted — keep geometric, not decorative
- Pauldrons: wide, flat-topped, heavy — the horizontal counterweight to the sword diagonal
- Chest armor: single vertical seam, one horizontal band — minimal

**Material language:**
- Smooth plate, not spiky or ornate
- No exposed joints or mechanical parts visible
- No skin or fabric gaps at core body — armor is total

**Color distribution:**
- Armor: porcelain white to silver-grey range
- Seams / structural detail: silver `#a0a0b0` or very subtle violet `#3d2e55`
- No warm tones in armor — cool white only

---

## 5. Sword Rules

> The Monolith Sword is not a weapon. It is an object of mass.

**Form:**
- Perfectly rectangular slab — no taper, no point, no curved edge
- Width: approximately 1/4 of total body height when held
- Length: taller than the character when vertical
- Carried diagonally — tip near ground, grip near right shoulder

**Material:**
- Pure matte black — same void register as the palette `#050508`
- No shine, no energy glow, no runes in default state
- Single edge-catch glint in silver/white is permitted (1px highlight) — structural, not magical

**Proportional weight:**
- The sword should feel heavier than a person could reasonably carry — this is the point
- Guard: solid horizontal rectangular bar — no elaborate crossguard design

**Forbidden on sword:**
- Organic blade curve
- Glowing blade or energy channel (unless explicitly in a variant scene)
- Thinning toward tip — stays rectangular to the end
- Named sword — do not assign personal name without canon decision

## 6. Forbidden Drift List

The following directions will drift Mikage from canon identity. Do not permit:

| Drift | Why Forbidden |
|---|---|
| Eye slit or glowing eyes on helmet | Humanizes — removes faceless sacred core |
| Warm palette (gold, orange, cream) in armor | Moves toward hero/divine — wrong register |
| Organic flowing hair as primary visual identity | Shifts to fantasy character archetype |
| Thinning / tapered sword | Loses monolith weight, becomes conventional blade |
| Spiky or ornate armor panels | Moves toward anime battle archetype |
| Demon horns or skull motifs | Wrong axis entirely — not dark, not evil |
| Smiling or emotional expression of any kind | Antithetical to sealed identity |
| Mechanical / robotic exposed joints | Moves toward mech — Mikage is sacred-tech not mech |
| Red accent color | Too aggressive / conventional villain read |
| Symmetrical front-facing pose by default | Prefer diagonal stance — sword establishes angle |
