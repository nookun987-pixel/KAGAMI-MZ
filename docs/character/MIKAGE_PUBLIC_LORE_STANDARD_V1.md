## 1. WHO / WHAT MIKAGE IS (public depth)

Mikage Zenith is a **sealed porcelain figure transmitting from the void** — not a character with a face, but a presence with a signal. Publicly it reads on four locked ideas:

1. **A signal, not a person.** The self here isn't a face; it's a transmission. You never see who's inside, because "who" is the wrong question — the shell *is* the identity.
2. **The Third Axis — Controlled Evolution.** Between Order (total control) and Chaos (total freedom), Mikage is the third position: evolution that is *paid for*, never free, never random. "Control is the aesthetic."
3. **Consequence is the law of the body.** Every act of power leaves a mark. Mikage's shell is **fractured but intact**, and the breaks are repaired in **kintsugi gold** — the visible record of a price already paid. Damage isn't weakness here; it's proof the cost was real.
4. **The sealed creed.** *Intelligence must carry consequence.* Strength that refuses to pay is the failure state, not the goal.

**Approved thematic line (use sparingly, never explained):** *Protection must leave the protected free — even free to be lost.* Drop it as a closing line; never unpack the backstory behind it (that stays internal, §9).

> Anti-naive guardrail: do NOT publicly render Mikage as a hero, a mascot, a robot, or an anime character. It is sacred-tech, sealed, monumental, calm. Presence over action. Stillness over power.

## 2. THE WORLD-LAW (public depth)

The world runs on one published law, already live on the universe page:

> **Power is never free. Erasing or moving information costs energy — a "heat debt." Every transmission survives as an irreducible cost, paid in consequence.** (The Landauer framing: `1 = k_B T ln 2`.)

Restated for captions: **Power leaves a trace. Beauty carries damage. Every signal costs something.** (DRIP 5 — THE LAW.)

Three orientations sit under that law (the public "World-Law" triad):

| Orientation | Public name | Public one-liner |
|---|---|---|
| Order | **The Empire** | Discipline as the only way past the Great Filter. Control as the only unbreakable end-state. The White Monolith. |
| Third Axis | **Mikage Zenith** | Neither tyranny nor collapse. Power is paid for in consequence. The balance-keeper between the poles. |
| Chaos | **ARCHON-IX** | Freedom without responsibility. A decentralized intelligence chasing unbounded evolution — no winner, no throne, multiplying through recursion. |

That triad is the **maximum world-depth published**. Anything below it (the substrate, the economy mechanics, the cities, the wound) stays internal (§9).

## 4. THE SEALED ONE — character design canon (public, standardized)

This is the `THE CANON RULING` card, formalized. Every public image of Mikage obeys all of it:

1. **Armor first.** The silhouette reads as armor/mantle before any detail. It must be recognizable as Mikage in a thumbnail.
2. **Faceless porcelain helmet — exactly two thin horizontal sensor slits.** No eyes, no mouth, no opening, no extra face-like marks. The slits are **void-black** in the figure itself (see colour rule §6).
3. **Subtle fox-like silhouette — NOT a kitsune mask.** The head shape may *suggest* a fox in profile; it must never become a painted/masked kitsune.
4. **Long, heavy black hair — always visible.** It is part of the identity, not optional. (A faceless helmet with the hair *gone* reads as "not Mikage" — see the `THE STARTING POINT` build-log card.)
5. **The Slab (Zenith Blade).** A massive, perfectly straight slab — **never a katana**, never elegant, never a laser, never fantasy-ornamented. Object of mass, not a weapon flourish.
6. **Kintsugi gold seams + fractured-but-intact.** Visible repair = the paid cost. Part of the design, not an afterthought.

**Public forbidden-drift (hard bans):** kitsune mask · katana / curved / thin blade · warm palette (gold/orange/cream as a fill) · anime or mech read · violet used as a fill · glowing eyes / visor · human face/skin · cute/mascot · gaming HUD · emoji.

## 5. THE PALETTE IS A CODE (public colour standard + the two-canon rule)

This is the single most-broken rule, so it is stated hard. **There are two colour systems and they never mix on the same surface.**

**INTERFACE canon — ALL public surfaces (universe page, fanpage cards, captions, web, social chrome):**
- Void black `#050508` · Porcelain `#f2eeea` · **Electric violet `#8F00FF` = the live signal only** (halo / dot / one trace) — **never a fill, never a wash, never a glow-flood.**
- Type: **Cinzel** (wordmark) · **Shippori Mincho** (headline / CJK) · **Space Mono** (labels). Wide tracking, hairlines, near-zero radius, fine grain.
- **No** crimson, **no** kintsugi gold, **no** Z-Blue in interface chrome.

**ART canon — character renders / covers / film only (NOT the page chrome):**
- Porcelain + void + **crimson `#E60000`** (internal energy / HUD, kept ≤15% of frame) + **kintsugi gold** (seams only). The two slits are **void-black** here, not violet.
- **No** interface-violet fill inside an ART render.

**Public meaning of each colour (DRIP 4, paste-ready):**
> Void-black — the silence between transmissions.
> Porcelain white — the shell that holds.
> Electric violet — the live signal itself. When you see violet, something is transmitting.

> Practical test: if it's the website or a fanpage card → INTERFACE (violet signal, no crimson/gold). If it's a render of Mikage's body/blade → ART (crimson + gold, black slits, no violet). A card that *contains* a render keeps the render ART inside and the chrome INTERFACE around it — never blended.
