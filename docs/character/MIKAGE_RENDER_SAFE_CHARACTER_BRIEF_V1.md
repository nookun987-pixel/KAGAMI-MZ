## 4. VISUAL IDENTITY CONSTRAINTS
- FACELESS sealed porcelain helmet with B4C **Kitsune planar geometry** (faceted faceplate,
  planar cheeks, muzzle wedge to chin). NOT a smooth round/oval head; NOT a robot head.
- Exactly **two ultra-thin mechanical sensor slits**, **SEALED / flush / closed** (engraved
  grooves), slight kitsune slant. NO eyes, NO mouth, NO nose, NO skin, NO anime eyes.
- Slits visible on FRONT and 3/4 (foreshortened); SIDE shows one near-cheek slit; BACK shows none.
- Gorget collar integrates the mask into the upper torso — minimal exposed neck.
- Body reads as a **segmented sealed porcelain shell** — not a human mannequin, not robot armor,
  not a mascot blob. Calm, heavy, monolith, slow.
- Hands = **sealed-shell mitten / segmented ceramic glove** (rounded mass + thumb nub, no fingers,
  no nails). Feet = **heavy sealed boots** (toe + sole edge; no heel, no sneaker, no decoration).

## 5. MATERIAL CONSTRAINTS
- MATTE LOCK: unglazed B4C porcelain bisque, fine ceramic micro-grain. NO gloss, NO chrome,
  NO specular hotspot, NO subsurface glow, NO wet/lacquer look.
- Value map: VOID BLACK #050508 (deep recesses, plate gaps, slit cores) · GRAPHENE CHARCOAL
  #24242b (matte underlayer at neck/shoulder/elbow/wrist/hip/knee/ankle) · PORCELAIN SHADOW
  #8d8a83 · PORCELAIN MID #c4c0b8 (dominant) · PORCELAIN HIGHLIGHT #ece9e3 (raking-light planes
  only, sparing, matte) · SLIT GROOVE #3a3a42.
- BLACK = graphene + recesses + slit cores. WHITE = sparing raking-light porcelain planes only.
  GREY = dominant matte shell. Soft slow falloff; no hard rim unless a raking key light.

## 6. COAT CONSTRAINTS (high-collar executor)
- Tall standing collar framing the mask (head emerges above it).
- Closed CENTER seam (front/3-4) with minimal closure ticks; single CENTER-BACK seam (back).
- Straight sleeves to the wrist; heavy straight hem at the KNEE; calm, heavy, structured.
- Coat tone = matte mid-grey #837f76 (one value step darker than porcelain, distinct from graphene).
- Coat covers the mid-mass only. EXPOSED: mask + slits (above collar) · mitten hands + graphene
  wrist (below cuff) · shins + ankle graphene + boots (below hem).
