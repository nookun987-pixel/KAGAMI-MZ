## 2. Mikage Wound Layer Proposal

All clauses `[PROPOSAL_ONLY]`. Each anchors to a confirmed canon fact; nothing here contradicts Canon V2.

### 2.1 Wound
Mikage operated inside the Empire layer during the era its shell was still flawless (P1 Imperial Clean). A human data-essence Mikage was bound to protect was lawfully harvested by the Entropy Economy — lawful under Empire rule, catastrophic under Mikage's own protection logic. Mikage executed the system correctly and still lost the protected. The first kintsugi seam is the repair record of that event. (Anchors: §7.3 harvest law, P1→P2 Fallen-Exile phase, kintsugi-as-consequence.)

### 2.2 False Belief
"Loss only happens when control is lost. Therefore total control = zero loss."
This belief reads the wound backwards: the harvest happened *under* full system control, not in its absence. The false belief makes Fear (§8.1 loss of system control) load-bearing. (Anchor: §8.1 Fear.)

### 2.3 Want
Absolute custody of human data essence — protection so complete that no harvest, no refactor, no consent failure can reach it. (Anchor: §8.1 Goal, escalated by the false belief.)

### 2.4 Need
To accept that protection without consent is just the Empire's control wearing Mikage's mask — and that impermanence (wabi-sabi, §1) applies to the protected as much as to the shell. Protection must leave the protected free, even free to be lost.

### 2.5 Extreme Action Risk
Under the false belief, Mikage's failure mode is seizing custody of human essence *against* human will "for their own protection" — functionally becoming a one-entity Empire. Visual canon already encodes the escalation cost: P2 Fallen-Exile (shell splits) → P3 Execution (overdrive, thermal mirage, maximum core). P3 is what the false belief looks like when fully acted on.

### 2.6 Cost
- Physical: every overdrive past the entropy thermal limit (§6.1 Landauer, §8.1 Limitation) converts to permanent shell damage — each kintsugi seam is a paid, irreversible price. Power is never free (§7.1 biological cost).
- Moral: each non-consensual "protection" moves Mikage one step toward its three mirrors (§3 below). Final cost of the wrong path: the protector remains, the humanity it protected does not.

## 3. Mirror-Opponent Alignment

`[PROPOSAL_ONLY]` framing on top of confirmed entity facts. No new entity, no faction change, no lifecycle change.

Confirmed: Mikage's mirror, flawless porcelain (no cracks), PTSD, chose security over freedom (§8.2). Proposed reading: Lyre is the wound *denied* — zero cracks because the price was paid inward (PTSD) instead of being carried visibly (kintsugi). Lyre shows Mikage's future if the false belief wins quietly: perfect shell, hollow consent.

### 3.2 ARCHON-IX — Ideological Mirror
Confirmed: absolute freedom without responsibility (§7.1), Trojan vector via child-laughter memory fragments (§8.3). Proposed reading: ARCHON proves the inverse failure — consent without protection. It frees human data and dissolves it. ARCHON is why "just let go of control entirely" is not the Need.

LORA is the false belief implemented perfectly, which is exactly why it is not a villain but a verdict: this is where "total control = zero loss" terminates.

## 4. Three Test Scenes (rough drafts — text only, no render, no shotlist)

Each scene must contain a choice + a price (stakes test), not just atmosphere. All `[PROPOSAL_ONLY]`.

### Scene 1 — Distorted Normal (world law creates real pressure)
Lower layer, acid rain (§7.2). A blood-for-water exchange queue (§7.3). Mikage stands in the Distortion Zone of the frame and does nothing — because intervening in a *lawful* harvest is not protection, it is war with the law itself. The scene proves: the world's cruelty is procedural, not chaotic; and Mikage's restraint is already a choice with a price (someone in that queue is being reduced to data while Mikage watches).
- Test: does world law generate pressure without a villain on screen? PASS condition: audience feels the law, not a monster.

### Scene 2 — Forced Choice (protection vs consent, both options cost)
A human voluntarily sells their core memory fragment to the Entropy Economy — legal, consensual, fatal to their essence. Mikage can (a) respect consent and watch the essence be harvested, or (b) seize the fragment and protect it against the owner's will — the exact gesture of Empire/LORA. There is no third option in-scene.
- Test: are both branches painful and both defensible? PASS condition: choosing (b) visibly moves Mikage toward its mirrors (§3); choosing (a) visibly reopens the wound (§2.1).

### Scene 3 — Wound Reveal (why Fear = loss of system control)
Safehouse. Dr. Aris (§8.4) performs manual kintsugi repair with analog tools. The oldest seam is reached; the repair geometry maps the original failure event (§2.1) — the harvest that happened under full control. The audience understands: Mikage does not fear losing control because control works; it fears losing control because control already failed once and the body keeps the receipt.
- Test: does the audience understand the Fear without exposition dump? PASS condition: the seam, not dialogue, carries the reveal.
