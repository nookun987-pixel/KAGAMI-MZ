- A beat is assigned only where lyric evidence and/or a locked anchor supports placement. Mood-only material remains `MOTIF_ONLY` or `ATMOSPHERE_ONLY`; insufficient material remains `UNMAPPED`.
- Evidence excerpts below are verbatim from the listed source. `<br>` represents a source line break.
- Confidence follows the operator scale. `CONFIRMED` requires a direct locked link; thematic fit alone never receives it.
- `PUBLIC/HELD sensitivity` is a narrative-use boundary, not a release-status ruling.
