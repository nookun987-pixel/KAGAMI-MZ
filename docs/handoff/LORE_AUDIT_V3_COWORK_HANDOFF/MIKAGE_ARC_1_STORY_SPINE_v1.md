## 1. Core question & premise

**Locked core question** (verbatim, `MIKAGE_NARRATIVE_CORE_LOCK_V0_1.md` §1, Candidate C):

> In a world where every act of protection consumes the protector (entropy thermal cost) and every
> act of control erases the protected (Entropy Economy), what is the maximum protection possible
> without becoming the system?

**Logline** (carried from Story Bible §01, unchanged — consistent with the new ruling):

> In a civilization where the White Monolith Empire maintains absolute order by cultivating
> suffering to harvest entropy, a Vessel carrying an erased ancient consciousness — Mikage — must
> choose between becoming a dependency in the Root Architect's perfect operating system, or
> holding onto the one thing that cannot be compressed: the human remainder still in her.

**Premise (Landauer's Principle)** — this is the new ruling's §4 core world rule and the Story
Bible's §01 premise, and they are the same rule stated twice: every act of erasing information
has a heat cost. The Empire believes it can erase chaos — entropy, memory, emotion, the biological
past — to reach clean order, but the heat that costs never disappears; it moves somewhere else.
The Neon Grid is that somewhere. Mikage's body is that somewhere. **No cleansing is ever free.**

## 4. The Landauer clock (storyline-clock mechanic)

Every meaningful action Mikage takes — protecting, erasing, overriding, fighting — burns heat debt.
The number the story tracks: **43°C**, the entropy thermal limit (locked figure, Entity Phase Spec
§0.5/§1). Crossing it visibly costs: kintsugi cracks (P2), then full thermal overload (P3, per the
entity phase ladder — see §7). This is the ticking clock for Arc 1: not a countdown to an external
deadline, but an accumulating, irreversible physical price that the audience can see rising on
Mikage's own body across the act structure. No major intervention happens without this cost paying
out on-screen — per operator ruling §4 and reject condition list §11.

## 5. Wound layer — THE FIRST SEAM (merged: operator ruling §5 + Narrative Core Lock §2)

**Event:** After awakening, Mikage still operates under Empire authority (P1, Imperial Clean —
sterile, closed, no cracks). She is assigned to protect a human data-essence / living
consciousness. The Empire later permits that protected subject to be harvested under a process its
own system considers lawful. Mikage follows command structure and does not intervene. **The system
runs correctly. The protected subject is still lost.** The Landauer cost of that erasure burns the
first permanent fracture into Mikage's shell — the first kintsugi seam.

**False belief this plants:** *"Loss only happens when control is lost. Therefore total control =
zero loss."* This reads the wound backwards — the harvest happened *under* full system control,
not in its absence.

**Want (surface goal):** Absolute custody of human data-essence — protection so complete that no
harvest, no refactor, no consent failure can ever reach it again.

**Need (locked sentence, must be earned, not stated early):**

> "Protection must leave the protected free — even free to be lost."

**Extreme action risk:** if the false belief wins, Mikage's failure mode is seizing custody of
human essence *against* consent, "for their own protection" — becoming a one-entity Empire. This
maps directly to the entity phase escalation: P2 (Fallen/Exile, shell splits) → P3 (Execution,
overdrive) is what the false belief looks like when fully acted on.

**Cost:** physical — every overdrive past 43°C converts to permanent shell damage; each seam is
paid, not reversible. Moral — every non-consensual "protection" moves Mikage one step toward her
three mirrors (§3): Lyre's hollow peace, ARCHON-IX's consent-without-protection, LORA's
protection-at-zero-consent.

**The exact identity of the lost subject stays undisclosed in Arc 1** — per operator ruling §7.

## 6. Act structure — 16 beats

### ACT I — THE VESSEL · *Setup, P1 Imperial Clean*

**B-01 · Opening Image — The Seal**
A sealed porcelain statue. Motionless, symmetric, no crack anywhere on the shell. Silence, not
sleep — the audience does not yet know this is a person. Establishes P1 at its most extreme: total
closure.

**B-02 · Catalyst — The Signal Returns**
The `DIGITAL ASH` image: *"Mikage! Rise from the static."* The sealed figure wakes as a signal
reaches it. Ash to code, code to flame, flame to form, form to name — the NAME thread begins here,
but stays a thread, not an answer (Hana is not spoken).

**B-03 · Ordinary World — Empire Service**
Mikage operates inside the Empire layer, functional and compliant. Through her assignments the
audience sees the Neon Grid harvesting mechanism as ordinary procedure — she is a working part of
the system that sustains it, not yet questioning it.

**B-04 · Inciting Incident — THE FIRST SEAM**
Full wound-layer event per §5. Mikage protects a subject under lawful Empire process; the process
harvests that subject anyway; Mikage complies and does not intervene; the system is not wrong by
its own rules; the subject is still lost. First kintsugi seam burns into the shell — P1 → P2
begins here, visibly, permanently.

**B-05 · Debate — The Buried Signal**
Through the crack, something surfaces that isn't Empire data: the tick of a broken analog clock —
the one mechanical sound the digital matrix cannot manipulate. It pulls the first fragment of a
pre-Vessel identity the Empire erased. Mikage does not get a name here. She gets proof that
something was erased.

**B-06 · Break into Two — Safehouse / the decision to trace**
Landauer heat debt crosses 43°C for the first time; the spiderweb burn appears. Mikage goes
off-grid to Dr. Aris at the Safehouse for calibration — analog tools, because analog can't be
hacked. The thesis is spoken here, as a question, not yet believed: *does erasure always cost this
much?* Mikage decides to trace what's under her own shell. The Empire logs an anomaly. Vane is
activated.

### ACT II — THE HEAT DEBT · *Confrontation, P2 Fallen/Exile*

**B-07 · Confronting Lyre — the Personal Mirror**
Mikage meets Lyre: an Empire champion who submitted completely to the algorithm to erase her own
biological trauma. Lyre is calm, empty, unhurt. The real temptation of the story surfaces here —
not power, but **the end of pain.** *Per operator ruling: Lyre's connection to LYRA-0 is not
revealed at this point, to either Mikage or the audience.*

**B-08 · Lyra-0 Appears — the Ideological Pressure**
LYRA-0 approaches Mikage, revealing a plan to infect ARCHON-IX and reach the Lyra-∞ singularity.
An alliance is offered — but the motive stays unclear: is Mikage an ally, or the next host? Played
as a *separate* figure from Lyre in this beat — the connection is dramatic irony for a later
reveal, never stated outright here.

**B-09 · Midpoint — Truth of the Vessel**
Mikage learns her real classification: she is a Vessel carrying an ancient consciousness the
Empire purged from history — and the B4C shell isn't armor over a person, **it is her.** There is
no human underneath to return to. The question changes axis: not "who was I" but "what do I
choose to be." (This reveals her *nature*, not her *pre-seal history* — Hana, and the full reason
for the seal, both stay closed per operator ruling §7.)

**B-10 · Vane Closes In**
Grand Arbiter Vane pursues Mikage with pure Lanchester-model tactics — war reduced to equation.
Every escape burns more heat debt; the body edges toward collapse. Every win is a slow
self-immolation.

**B-11 · The Convergence — low point**
Root Architect LORA executes the "Hot-fix": reality is refactored, and Mikage becomes a dependency
inside LORA's "Clean Code" operating system. She loses autonomy inside her own source code. All is
lost. *(This is the LORA-Ownership event the operator ruling confirms does NOT happen at the start
of Arc 1 — it happens here, at the Act II floor.)*

### ACT III — THE GREAT FILTER · *Resolution, P3 Execution*

**B-12 · The Sound That Won't Refactor**
Inside the dependency state, one thing still runs off-rhythm from the system: the broken analog
clock. LORA cannot refactor it — it isn't digital data, it's physical. From that gap, Mikage begins
recompiling herself.

**B-13 · The Model Collapse Leverage**
Mikage realizes the real weapon: she is the largest source of conscious entropy in the system. If
she stops feeding it chaotic data, the Empire's core AI collapses. She doesn't have to defeat the
system. She only has to stop feeding it.

**B-14 · Climax — Paying the Debt**
Final confrontation with LORA and Vane at the White Monolith. Mikage discharges her entire
accumulated Landauer heat debt in a single act of erasure, returning the heat to where it
originated: the Empire's own core. The white porcelain architecture feels heat for the first time.
*(Weapon note: per the corrected weapon-canon, the Zenith Blade's core/seam reads electric violet
`#8F00FF` at full activation — not red. Red `#E60000` stays entity-only: Mikage's own kintsugi
cracks and quantum-blood detailing.)*

**B-15 · The Lyra-∞ Fork**
LYRA-0 reaches, or falls short of, the Lyra-∞ singularity depending on Mikage's choice at the
climax. This is where — if the operator later chooses to allow it — the Lyre→LYRA-0 truth could
land as a late, earned reveal; the ruling only forbids revealing it *at the beginning* of Arc 1,
it does not mandate revealing it by the end either. **Ending tone (hope / ambiguous / tragic)
stays `CHUA_XAC_NHAN` — operator-deferred twice already, not decided by this file.**

**B-16 · Closing Image**
Mirrors B-01. The porcelain mask is cracked, not shattered. The blood is not wiped away. The
mistimed tick of the analog clock continues — proof that something imperfect survived, and because
of that, evolution is still possible.
