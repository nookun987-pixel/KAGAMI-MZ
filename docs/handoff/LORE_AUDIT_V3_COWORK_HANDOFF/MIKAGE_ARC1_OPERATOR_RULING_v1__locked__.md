## 1. Protagonist Identity

- Common in-story name: `MIKAGE`
- Full in-story name: `MIKAGE ZENITH`
- Primary weapon: `ZENITH BLADE`
- Mikage Zenith is not a separate fictional entity from Mikage.

## 2. Arc 1 Initial State

- Mikage begins Arc 1 dormant and sealed inside a porcelain statue.
- Mikage has not yet entered LORA Ownership or any Phase 3 state.
- The opening awakening follows the established `DIGITAL ASH` image: the sealed porcelain figure
  wakes as the signal returns. (Source lyric confirms "Mikage wakes" and closes on "Mikage sleeps /
  Then wakes again".)

## 3. Primary Opposing Forces

Arc 1 uses two-sided pressure:

- `EMPIRE / THE WHITE MONOLITH` = the principal institutional antagonist and visible controlling
  power.
- `ARCHON / ARCHON-IX` = the destabilizing Chaos force exploiting fractures in the Empire system.
- Mikage is not aligned with either side. Mikage represents the Third Axis: Controlled Evolution.

The Empire drives the central conflict of Arc 1. ARCHON-IX increases the danger but does not
replace the Empire as the main antagonist.

## 4. Load-Bearing World Rule

The central world rule is the Landauer cost:

- No meaningful action is free.
- Information erasure, control, protection and power expenditure produce physical or systemic
  cost.
- Power must leave a trace.
- Damage and consequence cannot be removed merely because an action was lawful, efficient or
  well-intended.
- Any major intervention without a visible cost is a canon violation.

## 5. First Irreversible Change

**Event name:** `THE FIRST SEAM — FAILED PROTECTION`

After awakening, Mikage still operates under Empire authority and is assigned to protect a living
consciousness or data-essence. The Empire later permits that protected subject to be harvested
under a process considered lawful by its system. Mikage obeys the command structure and does not
intervene.

The system operates correctly. The protected subject is still lost.

The Landauer cost burns a permanent fracture into Mikage's porcelain shell, creating the first
seam. This event permanently breaks Mikage's belief that correct execution produces a correct
result. From this point onward, Mikage can no longer return to unconditional obedience.

The exact identity of the lost subject remains undisclosed in Arc 1.

**Operator's stated rationale (kept verbatim, not restated):** the event must simultaneously (a)
trigger the Landauer law, (b) put Mikage in direct opposition to the Empire, (c) create a lasting
physical wound, and (d) prove that "following the correct order" is not the same as "doing the
right thing." A source file already describes Mikage carrying out Empire procedure correctly while
still losing the protected subject, and the first seam is that event's own repair record — this
ruling formalizes that reading as Arc 1's inciting incident, it does not invent it from nothing.

## 6. Arc-1 Identity Rulings

**Mikage**
- `Mikage` and `Mikage Zenith` are the same protagonist.
- Mikage Zenith is the full name; Mikage is the normal in-story form.

**Lyre / LYRA-0**
- Lyre was an Empire / White Monolith champion.
- Vane was Lyre's superior commander.
- The Empire erased Lyre.
- LYRA-0 is the post-erasure state or re-coalesced existence of Lyre.
- `Commander Lyre` refers to Lyre before erasure.
- The Lyre → LYRA-0 truth is canon internally but must not be openly revealed at the beginning of
  Arc 1.

**LORA**
- LORA is a separate entity.
- LORA is the central server AI / machine intelligence of the Empire / White Monolith.
- LORA is not Lyre, LYRA, or LYRA-0.
- LORA's final motive remains unrevealed in Arc 1.

**Vane / Tai Vane**
- Vane is a living person.
- Vane is the Grand Arbiter and senior commander of the White Monolith.
- Vane directly commanded Lyre.
- `Tai Vane` is an alias, old designation, or archive-record name for Vane.
- Tai Vane is not a separate Archive Tower AI.

**ARCHON / ARCHON-IX**
- ARCHON-IX is the named Chaos antagonist appearing in Arc 1.
- The exact relationship between plain `ARCHON` and `ARCHON-IX` remains unresolved and must not be
  invented during script writing.

## 7. Information That Must Remain Unknown in Arc 1

The writer must not invent or fully explain:

1. Mikage's complete life or identity before being sealed.
2. The complete reason Mikage was sealed inside the statue.
3. The identity of the subject lost during `THE FIRST SEAM`.
4. LORA's ultimate objective.
5. The exact ontology of ARCHON and its relationship to ARCHON-IX.
6. The Lyre → LYRA-0 truth before its designated reveal.
7. A human face, normal human body, or confirmed biological identity underneath Mikage's sealed
   helmet.

These are intentional unknowns, not gaps the writer is authorized to fill.
