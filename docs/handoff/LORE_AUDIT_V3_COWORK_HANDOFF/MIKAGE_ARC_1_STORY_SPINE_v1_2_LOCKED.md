## 0. Ruling record — 2026-07-21, ARC 1 PROVISIONAL SPINE ITEMS

Operator ruling on the 6 items `MIKAGE_ARC_1_STORY_SPINE_v1_1_PATCH.md` §3 flagged
`PROVISIONAL_SPINE`. Recorded verbatim in substance, not paraphrased down:

### 0.1 World Geography — `LOCKED_FOR_ARC_1`

Arc 1 uses three world layers:
- `WHITE MONOLITH` — the Empire's upper-tier command layer.
- `THE NEON GRID` — the lower tier, where the cost of entropy and the consequences of order are
  pushed down onto.
- `EARTH / POST-GREAT FALL` — the layer of erased memory and Mikage's deeper origin.

Earth exists in internal canon but is **not opened as a main story thread in Arc 1.**

### 0.2 Neon Grid Entropy Mechanism — `LOCKED_INTERNAL_CANON`

The Empire does not merely neglect the Neon Grid. It actively sustains conditions of suffering and
instability to harvest entropy, emotional data, and informational heat for the White Monolith
system.

**Presentation rule:** do not explain this mechanism through exposition or long dialogue. The
audience must recognize it through the harvesting process, the environment, and consequences shown
on-screen. Do not use the word "farm" in public-facing dialogue for any character who does not yet
have access to the system-level truth.

### 0.3 LORA Convergence / Hot-fix Placement — `LOCKED_AT_B11`

LORA executes the Convergence / Hot-fix at B-11, the Act II floor. Mikage becomes a dependency
inside Clean Code and temporarily loses autonomy. This is LORA's first shift from systemic pressure
to direct intervention. **LORA Ownership does not appear before B-11.** LORA's ultimate objective
stays unrevealed.

### 0.4 Vessel Truth Reveal Timing — `LOCKED_PARTIAL_REVEAL_AT_B09`

At B-09, Mikage learns:
- She is classified as a Vessel.
- The B4C shell is not armor worn over a body — it is her current body.
- There is no ordinary human body underneath to remove the shell and return to.

B-09 does **not** reveal:
- The name Hana.
- Her life before being sealed.
- The full reason she was sealed.
- The identity of the ancient consciousness.
- Who created or placed that consciousness into the Vessel.

This is a reveal about present nature, not a complete origin reveal.

### 0.5 Lyra-∞ Ending — `HELD_OPEN`

Not locked: whether LYRA-0 reaches Lyra-∞ or not. B-15 must continue to support at least two
branches: (a) the singularity is prevented or fails, or (b) the singularity occurs but leaves an
unclosed consequence. Must be ruled before final Act III script and the final episode lock.

### 0.6 Hana Reveal Timing — `HELD_BEYOND_ARC_1`

Hana is the locked internal-canon pre-Vessel name. Within Arc 1: the name is not spoken, not
printed, not confirmed on any screen, record, HUD, or caption. Only the motif of an erased name,
the analog signal, and incomplete memory may be used. The reveal timing decision is deferred to a
later arc's ruling.
