## 11. Minimum Usable Mikage Production Asset Stack

Minimum stack before any film/video/shotlist/render work:

1. Locked identity reference set: key visual, helmet front, helmet side, blade.
2. Reviewed Phase 4 component set: helmet faceplate, sensor slit, armor/material language, underlayer, blade reference, UI/system language.
3. Character presence asset: bust or upper-body plate consistent with locked identity.
4. Body continuity requirement: clear decision whether full body is needed for the first proof target.
5. Environment/world plate requirement: at least one world-establishing visual or approved environment reference.
6. Event/system plate requirement: one non-character change/system beat or prop/event reference.
7. Motion readiness constraints: what can move, what must stay static, and what is forbidden.
8. Audio decision: silence, temp internal reference, or rights-cleared sound source.
9. QA gate: explicit checklist that prevents candidates from being called production-ready without evidence.
