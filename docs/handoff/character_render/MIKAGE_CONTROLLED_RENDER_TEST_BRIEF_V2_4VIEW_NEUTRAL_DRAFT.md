Create one neutral full-body character still of Mikage Zenith as a draft reference test: faceless sealed white porcelain B4C / Kitsune planar helmet, exactly two sealed sensor slits, no eyes, no mouth, no nose, no skin, no human face. Long heavy black hair falls behind the helmet and shoulders past the hip. Female-coded silhouette, strictly non-sexual, calm and ceremonial, no breast emphasis, no waist emphasis, no glamor or fanservice.
