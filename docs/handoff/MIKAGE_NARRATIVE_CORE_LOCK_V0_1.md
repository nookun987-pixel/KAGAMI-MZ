## 1. CORE QUESTION — LOCKED (Candidate C)

> **In a world where every act of protection consumes the protector (entropy thermal cost) and every act of control erases the protected (Entropy Economy), what is the maximum protection possible without becoming the system?**

Retained support lines (NOT the lock):

- Character moral question for dialogue/scene use (ex-Candidate B): *"If preserving human data essence requires violating human consent, can Mikage still be called a protector?"*

## 2. MIKAGE WOUND LAYER — LOCKED

### 2.1 Wound
Mikage operated inside the Empire layer during the era its shell was still flawless (P1 Imperial Clean). A human data-essence Mikage was bound to protect was lawfully harvested by the Entropy Economy — lawful under Empire rule, catastrophic under Mikage's own protection logic. Mikage executed the system correctly and still lost the protected. The first kintsugi seam is the repair record of that event.

### 2.2 False Belief
"Loss only happens when control is lost. Therefore total control = zero loss."
The belief reads the wound backwards: the harvest happened *under* full system control, not in its absence. It makes Fear (Canon V2 §8.1: loss of system control) load-bearing.

### 2.3 Want
Absolute custody of human data essence — protection so complete that no harvest, no refactor, no consent failure can reach it.

### 2.4 Need — WORDING LOCK
To accept that protection without consent is just the Empire's control wearing Mikage's mask — and that impermanence (wabi-sabi) applies to the protected as much as to the shell.

> **LOCKED SENTENCE: "Protection must leave the protected free, even free to be lost."**

### 2.5 Extreme Action Risk
Under the false belief, Mikage's failure mode is seizing custody of human essence *against* human will "for their own protection" — functionally becoming a one-entity Empire. Escalation maps to the locked entity phases: P2 Fallen-Exile (shell splits) → P3 Execution (overdrive). P3 is what the false belief looks like when fully acted on.

### 2.6 Cost
- Physical: every overdrive past the entropy thermal limit converts to permanent shell damage — each kintsugi seam is a paid, irreversible price. Power is never free (Third Axis: biological cost).
- Moral: each non-consensual "protection" moves Mikage one step toward its three mirrors (§3). Final cost of the wrong path: the protector remains, the humanity it protected does not.
