## 2. Operator Design Decision — Material Standard Amendment

DECISION (stated by operator): The Mikage bust / upper-body bridge surface is a **smooth monocoque porcelain shell** — a single continuous matte-white surface with a central seam and clean silhouette. The earlier requirement for visible B4C porcelain panel-gap seams and graphene underlayer showing through gaps is **NOT required** for the bust bridge and is set aside for this asset.

Amendment to `MIKAGE_BUST_UPPER_BODY_BRIDGE_ASSET_REQUEST_SPEC_V1.md`:
- Section 4.1 material rows ("B4C porcelain panel plate" + "graphene underlayer visible through gaps"): downgraded from REQUIRED to OPTIONAL for the bust bridge slot.
- New required material standard: matte-white smooth monocoque porcelain; cold, severe, non-human; no gloss/chrome.
- Section 9 checklist: the two checks "B4C porcelain material match" and "graphene underlayer match" are replaced by a single check "smooth matte porcelain monocoque, no gloss/chrome".

All other constraints of the spec (faceless hard-stop §6.1, silhouette, no anime/fashion drift, forbidden sources §7) remain fully in force.

### 2.1 Refine Policy (binding for any future pass on this asset)

PRINCIPLE: **smooth primary, detail secondary.** Any further refine of the bust bridge MAY only:
- keep the form/silhouette unchanged,
- keep the smooth matte porcelain surface as the dominant read,
- add at most very light micro-seams.

Any further refine MUST NOT:
- add large/structural armor panels,
- expose graphene clearly,
- create any black slit / visor / opening (faceless hard-stop still absolute).

Intent: a Mikage helmet/bust that is instantly recognizable by silhouette, absolutely faceless, not "detailed for the sake of detail" — fewer details, stronger brand identity. Detail must never override the minimal silhouette.
