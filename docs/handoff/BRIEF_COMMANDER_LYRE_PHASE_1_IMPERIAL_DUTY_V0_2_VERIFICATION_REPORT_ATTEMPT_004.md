## 5. ROOT-CAUSE READ (Cowork diagnostic, not a recommendation)

V0.2's redesign successfully addressed three of V0.1's failure modes by front-loading positive shape cues. Three new failure clusters remain:

**A. flux/dev pose default (largest remaining issue).** The model has a strong baseline "humanoid robot at rest, arms down" pose. V0.2's pose paragraph included both positive ("both hands clasped strictly behind the back") and explicit negation ("no hands at sides, no hands forward, no action pose"). The model honored the WEAPON anti-cues ("no pistol, no gun") but not the HAND-POSITION cue. POSE prompts on flux/dev are known to be weakly weighted relative to body/material cues.

**B. flux/dev small-detail omission.** Wrist-scale attachments (the ~15cm disc emitter on the forearm; the slim cylindrical Monowire hilt on the hip) are systematically dropped by flux/dev when the rest of the figure is detailed. This is a known limitation. Increasing prompt emphasis does not reliably solve it — typically requires reference-image conditioning or LoRA-style training.

**C. flux/dev "embellishment" defaults.** Hip-groin amber glow inserts are a common flux/dev sci-fi-robot embellishment that appears unprompted. V0.2 banned amber explicitly in F4 — the model produced amber anyway. This is similar to the amber-knee-light pattern in V0.1 attempt_004: amber is a flux/dev habit, location varies.

These are observations only. Cowork does NOT auto-edit, does NOT swap model, does NOT draft V0.3.
