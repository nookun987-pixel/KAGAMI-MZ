## 5. ROOT-CAUSE READ (Cowork's diagnostic, not a recommendation)

Two contributing causes, layered:

**A. Model bias (largest contribution).** `fal-ai/flux/dev` has a strong baseline toward "humanoid robot with breast cups + hip flare + amber eye points + decorative panel lines" when given any prompt about a humanoid figure, even with explicit anti-cues. Multiple compact-negative terms (`female body emphasis`, `breasts`, `hourglass waist`, `mecha clutter`, `gundam`, `glossy plastic`) were ignored. This is a known flux/dev failure mode on industrial-aesthetic prompts.

**B. Compact prompt design (smaller contribution).** The V0.1 compact prompt is well-structured but leans on negation ("not a heroine, not a pin-up"). Models that operate primarily on positive cue strength may anchor on "tall sealed androgynous imperial enforcement figure" as still "humanoid robot" rather than the specific industrial-block silhouette spec'd. Explicit POSITIVE shape cues ("flat rectangular chest plate," "no chest contour," "vertical column body, no waist taper") would force the silhouette more than negation alone.

Also: the compact prompt did not visually emphasize the disc emitter + Monowire hilt strongly enough — they appear late in the text. A future compact prompt that places weapon/shield specs prominently and uses concrete spatial language ("disc strapped flat across the left forearm like a circular bracer") may carry better through flux/dev.

These are observations, not actions. Cowork does NOT auto-edit the compact prompt and does NOT change `model_id`. Both are operator decisions.
