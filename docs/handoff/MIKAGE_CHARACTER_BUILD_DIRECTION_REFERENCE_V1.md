## 0. CORE PRINCIPLE (what makes it "correct")

Mikage = **Vessel** — a porcelain shell containing an ancient consciousness that the Empire purged. Every design choice must read as: **ordered shell outside, living / entropy leaking from inside.** That tension is the master constraint; all detail serves it.

---

## 1. HEAD / FACE

- Canonical face = **faceless porcelain helmet**, exactly **2 horizontal sensor slits**, graphene underlayer exposed at neck / joints. No eyes, nose, mouth.
- Expression is carried entirely by: **slit light (violet pulse)**, head tilt, orbital ring. No face → emotion lives in light + posture. This is where most drawings fail (adding a face breaks it).
- Film layer MAY overlay **Kitsune-mask + violet orbital ring** as a halo for cinematic shots.

**RECONCILE vs LOCKED canon (§2 of entity spec):**
- Locked: the 2 sensor slits ARE the film mask's 2 eye-slits, and they are **SEALED (sealed monocoque, 0.7", no open holes)**; vision routes through a graphene + side-channel BMF sensor beneath the shell. ✅ Compatible — "violet pulse" is read as a **brand/signal light treatment over sealed slits**, not an open optical aperture. Keep slits sealed; violet is emitted signal, not a hole.
- Kitsune mask: RESOLVED 2026-06-02 → the **Kitsune planar-geometry IS the mask form itself** (not an overlay). The cinematic halo = **violet orbital ring only**. See §8 D4.

## 5. MOTION / DEMEANOUR (cinematic)

- Slow, heavy, deliberate. **Static > dynamic.** The Vessel is ancient and heavy.
- Comes alive through: head tilt, **slit breathing (violet breathing)**, orbital rotation, light drift of hair / coat.

## 6. HARD "DON'T" LIST (to avoid "worse than anime")

- No human face / eyes / skin on the brand mark — **the helmet IS the face.**
- No anime cute / moe. This is cold, monumental, funereal — not adorable.
- No neon clutter, no game HUD, no violet wash.

- Build the character on the **film / art canon** (Kitsune / coat / blade / kintsugi / Z-Blue) for MV / teaser / character art.
- **Spotify Canvas + UI** use only the **reduced brand version**: faceless helmet + void / porcelain / violet-signal. Same character, two registers — do not let the warm / cinematic treatment leak into Canvas.
- ✅ Matches LOCKED canon §2: brand mark and film entity are ONE identity at two fidelities; "keep layers separate" = don't let one invent the other's canon, NOT that they look different.
