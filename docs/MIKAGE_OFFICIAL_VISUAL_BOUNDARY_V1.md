# MIKAGE — OFFICIAL VISUAL BOUNDARY (CANON AMENDMENT V1)
*Locked by operator, 2026-06-30. Sits beside the brand canon and the Fandom Positioning doc. Governs where anime aesthetics are allowed and where they are banned.*

---

## THE RULE (immutable)

> **MIKAGE OFFICIAL VISUAL BOUNDARY**
> Official Mikage artwork remains **cinematic sculptural realism.**
> Anime and cel-styled interpretations belong to the **fan and editorial layer only.**
> Mikage must remain **faceless**: no human eyes, mouth, nose, or expressive anime face.
> The **porcelain helmet, two sensor slits, black body mass, and violet signal** are immutable identity marks.

---

## WHY (the call)

The cyber-dark / nightcore / Gen-Z fan-art crowd overlaps heavily with anime-edit / AMV culture. We want that audience — but Mikage must not dissolve into one of a thousand generic AI-anime characters. So we split the two jobs instead of forcing one style to do both:

- **Official identity** = dark, cinematic, sculptural, mysterious. The anchor that stays the same for years.
- **Spread language** = anime edit, AMV, speed-lines, cel-shading, fan-art, lore theory. The viral surface.

Because Mikage is **faceless**, the usual anime drift risk (moe faces, big eyes) is structurally blocked — there is no anime face to draw. The helmet silhouette holds the line on its own.

---

## APPLY IT

| Surface | Rule |
|---|---|
| Cover art, hero render, website, character reveal, reference sheet | **Realism only** |
| Reels / TikTok / Shorts (motion) | Anime-edit **grammar allowed**: speed-ramp, speed-lines, glitch, hard cut, glow, kinetic typography |
| Fan art | **Anime-ify freely** — but keep helmet faceless + exactly two sensor slits |
| "Official Anime Mikage Version" | **Banned.** Does not exist. |

Supporting cast (Lyra-0, etc.) stays inside the **same cinematic world** — no separate anime universe.

---

## FAIL-SAFE

If anime fan-art gets near-zero engagement after ~6–10 test posts: **do not change canon.** Only raise the anime level in **video editing and framing**; the official Mikage model stays realism.
