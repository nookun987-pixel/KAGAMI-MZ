### 1.3 How it reads in frame

## 2. OPTION B — LAYERED BARRIER FIELD (PLASMA VENT)

### 2.1 What it is

NOT a physical object. A field-effect emission generated by a small cylindrical core device worn on Lyre's left forearm (~15 cm device). When activated, it projects a vertical white-and-cyan plasma curtain in front of her — multi-layered, semi-transparent, scaling from chest-height to over-head depending on threat. The "shield" is the **emission**, not the device.

### 2.2 Visual signature

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Core device | Small cylindrical emitter on Lyre's left forearm — brushed white ceramic, ~15 cm long, ~4 cm diameter, slim and almost vestigial |
| Plasma curtain (active) | Vertical band of pale-white-with-cold-cyan-edges, 5–7 visible layers stacked at ~2 cm depth each, slightly translucent (silhouette behind it readable but blurred); curtain face ~80 cm wide × 150 cm tall |
| Light emission | Curtain glows cold-cyan at edges, fades to white-near-Lyre; NO warm light; NO purple; NO red |
| Heat | Cool — emission does not generate heat distortion (contrast to Mikage's Zenith Blade thermal overload) |
| Behavior | When struck, the impacted layer flashes brighter and dissipates; subsequent layers absorb remaining force; can sustain ~5 impacts before core requires cooldown |
| Audio signature (downstream) | Low harmonic hum when active; high-frequency tic-tic when absorbing impact |

### 2.3 How it reads in frame

- Lyre extends left arm forward; cylindrical core glows, curtain materializes ~30 cm in front of arm
- Plasma curtain occupies the visual space where a physical shield would be
- When Mikage's Zenith Blade strikes the curtain, **one layer flashes white and vanishes**; remaining layers persist
- In static Phase 1 (not engaged), no curtain visible — only the small core device on forearm

### 2.4 Pros

- Matches Canon V2 §8.2 "vertical white/cyan plasma vent" language
- Lighter visual footprint when inactive (core device only)
- Strong "system-emission" feel — fits Empire's sacred-tech identity
- The multi-layer dissipation is **cinematic** (each layer breaking is a story beat)

### 2.5 Cons

- Harder to render consistently (plasma effects are stylistically variable across AI image tools)
- Risk of drifting into generic sci-fi forcefield / Iron Man arc reactor aesthetic
- Less photogenic in static frames (Phase 1 has no visible shield)
- Doesn't anchor "unbreakable" as a tangible thing — the unbreakability is abstract layer-stacking
